Traffic Management System

Welcome

New here? Fill in all three boxes and click Register. Already registered? Enter your username and password and click Login.

Low Medium High

Click a circle on the map to see the road name, congestion and density. Bigger circles mean heavier traffic.

Plan a route

Choose where you start and where you want to go. The route avoids busy roads when it can.

Report an incident

Tell other drivers about an accident, roadblock or hazard.

Traffic alerts

Current congestion, accidents, closures and hazards.

Notifications

Tick the areas you care about and click Save. You will be told about events in those areas and the areas next to them.

Emergency vehicle priority

Enter your vehicle ID and trip. You get the fastest route and the signals on it turn green.
